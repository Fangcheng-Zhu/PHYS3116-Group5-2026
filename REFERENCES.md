# References

1. Faber, S. M., & Jackson, R. E. (1976). Velocity dispersions and mass-to-light ratios for elliptical galaxies. *The Astrophysical Journal*, **204**, 668–683. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1086/154215); [original article via NASA ADS](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1976ApJ...204..668F).
   Used as the historical context for the luminosity–dispersion relation and the classical exponent near four. Its measurements and sample are not identical to the present r-band SAMI analysis.

2. Nigoche-Netro, A., Aguerri, J. A. L., Lagos, P., Ruelas-Mayorga, A., Sánchez, L. J., & Machado, A. (2010). The Faber–Jackson relation for early-type galaxies: dependence on the magnitude range. *Astronomy & Astrophysics*, **516**, A96. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/200912719); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.5496).
   Supports the need to consider luminosity and magnitude range when comparing fitted relations across samples. It does not establish which factor explains the exponent in our data.

## Input data

The project analyses the four SAMI Option 2 CSV files supplied through PHYS3116 Moodle, retained in `row-data/`. No extra survey catalogue was added to the numerical pipeline.
